Calculate and visualize helium spectra near 1083 nm with Zeeman splitting for ³He and ⁴He isotopes. Adjust magnetic field, temperature and pressure to see real-time changes in the spectra.

Line positions and strengths come from a model valid to a few mbar. The pressure control broadens the line shape only: collisional shifts (~1.4 MHz/mbar) and any line mixing among the 2³P sublevels are not included, so above a few mbar the widths are right but the positions are still the low-pressure ones.

Further Information

Parameters:

  • B Field: 0.0001 - 10.0 Tesla
  • Temperature: 77 - 1000 Kelvin
  • Pressure: 0 - 300 mbar (collisional broadening only)
  • Doppler Width: ∝ √(T/300), Gaussian
  • Collisional Width: 12.0 (³He) / 10.4 (⁴He) MHz/mbar FWHM, Lorentzian

Polarizations:

  • σ+: Right circular polarization
  • σ-: Left circular polarization
  • π: Linear polarization

Physics:

  • Zeeman splitting in magnetic field
  • Hyperfine structure included
  • Doppler broadening from temperature
  • Collisional broadening from pressure (Voigt profile)

Transitions Table:

  • Groups lines within 2 GHz of a peak's intensity-weighted centroid; the frequency and wavelength shown are that centroid
  • Span shows each group's true extent, flagged when it exceeds the line width
  • Hover a transition arrow for its own frequency, intensity and share of the peak

Pumping readout:

  • With a row selected, each 2³S level shows how fast a 2 GHz laser on that peak's centroid empties it, relative to the levels the peak pumps
  • Other levels are labelled once they reach 0.1%; hover a lower level for the breakdown by line
  • Rates are per atom: level populations (spin temperature) are not included
